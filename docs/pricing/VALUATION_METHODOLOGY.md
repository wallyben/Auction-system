# Valuation methodology

1. Collect comps. Reject accessories, wrong generation (GM vs GM II, 4080 vs SUPER), bundles, graded-vs-raw mismatches, body-vs-kit.
2. Weight by evidence type × recency × territory × product/condition scores.
3. MAD outlier rejection; weighted median for expected sale.
4. Percentiles p10/p25/median/p75/p90.
5. Asking-only samples are haircut and confidence-capped. They are never labelled realised.
6. Display rounding follows confidence: thin evidence is shown as a range, not €824.17.
7. Calibration records exist by category/channel/condition/territory/confidence band. No correction is applied until sample size is honest.
