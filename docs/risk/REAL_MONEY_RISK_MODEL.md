# Real-money risk model

Cheap listings raise suspicion. High modelled spread is not a reason to buy.

Signals: weak identity, thin comps, too-cheap vs distribution, replica language, no images, lots, unknown seller, off-platform payment, obscured serial, stock photos, activation lock / console ban, fungus/haze.

`RISK_PASS` fails when any high flag is present or score ≥ 0.45. That blocks `BUY_READY`.

Remaining unresolved modes (red team): stolen-item certainty, marketplace ban after purchase, seller disappearance after off-platform deal, silent counterfeit of high-end GM glass, unobservable listing fraud, bulk saturation, return after Irish postage both ways.
